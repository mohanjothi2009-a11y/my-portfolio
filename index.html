Know Mohan

Beyond the resume — my background, my design philosophy, and what fuels my curiosity.

"I didn't take the traditional design school route. I study Electronics & Communication Engineering at MPNMJ Engineering College — but the moment I designed my first user interface, I was hooked."

Engineering taught me first-principles thinking, systematic problem-solving, and how to analyze complex constraints. UI/UX design gave me the creative canvas to turn that technical logic into empathy, clarity, and visual joy for everyday users.

"The best interfaces are the ones you never have to think about. They feel completely obvious in hindsight."

Right now, I spend my time balancing coursework with independent freelance projects and exploring how AI tools (like Claude, Midjourney, v0, and Cursor) can augment human design decisions — accelerating wireframes and prototypes without losing sight of user research and accessibility.
